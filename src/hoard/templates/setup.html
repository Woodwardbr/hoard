Welcome to Hoard

Hoard shows all your accounts in one place: net worth, spending, budgets, investments and loans. It runs entirely on this computer.

  • Your data stays here. It's saved in a file on this computer, not on anyone's server. There's no Hoard account to create.
  • It only reads. Hoard can see balances and transactions. It can't move money or pay bills.
  • Banks connect through Plaid, the service many finance apps use. You log in to your bank in Plaid's window; Hoard never sees your bank password.

Setup takes about 15 minutes, plus however long Plaid takes to approve your account (often quick, sometimes a day or two).

{# ---------------------------------------------------------------- 2 #}

Create your free Plaid account

Hoard uses your own Plaid account to connect to your banks. Plaid's free Trial plan covers up to 10 bank logins, which is plenty for a household.

  1. Open dashboard.plaid.com/signup and create an account with your email.
  2. When Plaid asks how you'll use it, choose Personal use.
  3. Answer the short questionnaire. Something like this is fine: "I'm using Hoard, a personal finance app that runs on my own computer, to see my own accounts. Nobody else's data is involved."
  4. Once your account is ready, find your keys in the Plaid dashboard under Developers, then Keys. You need the client_id and the Production secret.

Keep your secret private, like a password. Hoard stores it in your computer's secure password storage.

Add your first bank

Your keys work and are saved in secure storage.

Click Add a bank. Plaid opens a window where you:

  1. Search for your bank, card or brokerage.
  2. Log in with your usual username and password (some banks open their own site for this). Hoard never sees these.
  3. Choose which accounts to share, and agree.

Then Hoard pulls in balances and recent transactions, which can take a minute. Add as many banks as you like (up to 10 logins on Plaid's free plan).

    Skip for now. You can add banks later with Link Account at the top of every page.

    You're all set

    • Dashboard: net worth over time and this month's spending.
    • Budgets: set monthly limits per category and track them.
    • Portfolio: where your investments are, by sector.
    • Debt Tracker: loans, payments and when they'll be paid off.
    • Sync (top right) pulls the latest from your banks. Do it whenever you open Hoard.
    • Privacy (top right) hides every dollar amount, so you can show someone the app without showing your numbers.
    • Settings: reconnect a bank if it asks you to log in again, or change your Plaid keys.
